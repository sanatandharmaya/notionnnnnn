# Product

**Status:** source-backed synthesis, pending detailed cross-source review. See [source key](reports/key-sources.md) and [coverage](reports/reading-coverage.md).

Eternal Sanskriti is described as a multilingual Sanatan Dharma knowledge, devotional, editorial, and digital-services platform. Its goal is to publish sourced knowledge and support devotional practice without duplicating domain ownership. It addresses readers and learners, devotees managing private practice, and staff who author and review material. [K04, Executive Summary and §§1–2]

Human account types are **admin**, **devotee**, and **staff**. Editor, translator, reviewer, and similar functions are scoped capabilities, not additional account types. **Free**, **Seva**, and **Premium** are entitlement tiers, not permissions. [K04 §2; K05 D1-01, D1-12]

The primary journey is source intake → structured authoring → validation → independent review → translation and rights/safety review → approval → publication → public reading → correction or withdrawal. R1 proves this end to end for C01 Scriptures and C02 Bhakti; broader categories, personal state, commerce, events, and live experiences follow in later releases. [K04 §11; K07 R1–R5]

**Current exclusions and limits:** mobile is future optional scope; generic production Ask-AI, embeddings, and generative search are not approved; medical diagnosis/treatment is not implied by wellness or Ayurveda editorial content. The first release is a foundation, not the full product. [K06 lines 29, 122–128; K11 ADR-010; K07 R0]

Product outcomes and metrics require owner-approved definitions. No audience size, launch date, price, or performance target is established by the reviewed material. The [open questions](open-questions.md) cover decisions that require further evidence.
